Move In the rhythm What happens What it fixes
1 Before the rhythm Name the person and the moment. Who this is for, what they already know, and the situation in which they will need it. Written down in one line before anything else. Content built for an average learner who does not exist.
2 Before the rhythm Write the outcome as something observable, then choose the evidence. What the person will be able to do; what would show it. The activity is built to produce that evidence, not the other way round. Outcomes that cannot be assessed — "understand," "be aware of" — and activities that drift from what is finally marked.
3 Before the rhythm Choose the smallest form that works. Modality follows the task and the setting — what device, what time, what noise — not preference or precedent. What is left out is decided here and recorded. Courses where a checklist would do; video where a page would do.
4 Why & See Anchor it in the person's own case first. Open with their situation and the decision in front of them. Then the model — a worked example before the principle, because a principle without a case has nowhere to attach. Content that is accurate and never used.
5 Try Build the practice with its feedback. A task the person can attempt, get wrong, and see the consequence of, with feedback that says what to change rather than whether they were right. Retrieval and spacing are in the schedule, not left to habit. Passive exposure that feels like learning and does not consolidate.
6 Share Design the social layer. Where two or more people will meet the resource together, the exchange is structured — what to compare, what to explain to each other — so that expertise circulates rather than sitting with the confident. Discussion as filler; peer learning left to chance.
7 Reflect Close with transfer. A prompt that asks the person to name where, in their own work, this applies next — because that naming is what moves knowledge from the resource to the room. Learning that ends when the module does.
8 Throughout Draft accessible, write in plain language, test with the population. Access from the first draft, at the reading level of the people named in move 1, and a pass with real learners before release. What they cannot use is redesigned, not explained. Accommodation as a downstream fix; resources that fail and look like a learner who did not try.
